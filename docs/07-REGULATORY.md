# Regulatory tracking — out of scope

**PM decision, 2026-10-18.** This project does not track traffic law or regulatory compliance.
It is a business tool: candidates, hours, and money in and out.

An earlier version of this document researched Algerian driving-licence regulation in detail.
That was scope creep and it has been removed.

## What remains, and it is not regulatory

Licence **categories** and their **hour targets** are plain configuration on the school's
price list — the same kind of setting as a package price. The owner types in what he charges
and how many hours he gives. The app shows progress against that number. Nothing checks it
against any law, and nothing refuses an action because of one.

This is already the rule in `CLAUDE.md`:

> The app records what the school does; it does not enforce the law.

## If this ever comes back

Only on evidence that schools will pay for it — for example if a school owner says during a
visit that proving compliance is a real problem for him. Until then it is a distraction from
the money features that actually sell (`09-FIRST-RELEASE.md`).
